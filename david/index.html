Kingdom Quest — Shepherds Together (2–4 players)
On Xbox: each player holds ☰ Menu → “Use game controls” on their controller, presses Ⓐ to join, then any player presses ☰ Start to begin.
Move with the stick/D-pad · Ⓐ jump · Ⓧ sling the stone · stand by a fallen friend to revive them.
Player 1 can also use the keyboard: ← → move · Space jump · F sling · Enter start.

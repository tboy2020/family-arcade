How to Play 🦁

1
Take turns

Pass the device around. A banner shows whose turn it is, in their color.

2
Answer the question

Tap the animal's home, its food, or the right body part. Tap 🔊 to hear the question.

🔄
Life cycles

For a life cycle, tap the pictures in order — first, next, last!

💡
Stuck? Hint!

Tap 💡 for help. Wrong taps just wobble and say "try again" — never a buzzer!

Settings ⚙️

No data is collected and the game never goes online. Private & safe. 💛
